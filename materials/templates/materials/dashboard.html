🏛️ Executive overview

National Material Harmonization Platform

Finds duplicate material entries across India's public sector companies, confirms whether items are genuinely equivalent, and routes savings from shared inventory instead of repeat procurement.

ℹ️ Demo dataset — ONGC, NTPC, IOCL, SAIL, BHEL, BPCL
⇄ Try the comparator 🛡 Safety check
How it works — 6 steps
1. Ingest 📄
Legacy PSU data
PDFs, invoices, SAP/Oracle exports and multilingual catalogues
2. Parse ⚙️
OCR + NLP parser
Extracts grade, diameter, pressure rating and other structured attributes
3. Match 🧠
Semantic AI matching
Sentence-BERT vectors + attribute rule scoring
4. Validate 🛡️
Safety compliance check
Enforces NACE MR0175, ASME B16.34, API 6D before approving substitution
5. Decide ⚖️
Engineer sign-off
Human review with audit logging and active learning feedback
6. Save 🌱
Savings & ESG
Cross-PSU inventory transfer, cost avoidance and CO₂ reduction
