NATIONAL IMPACT & ROLE

India's Public Sector: Powering National Progress

Central Public Sector Enterprises (CPSEs) form the industrial backbone of India, fostering self-reliance, strategic capabilities, and sustainable socio-economic growth.

Established under government stewardship, Maharatna, Navratna, and Miniratna enterprises operate across vital sectors—from oil exploration and refining to power grids, heavy machinery, defence equipment, and aviation logistics.
